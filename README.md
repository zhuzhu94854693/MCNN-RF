The main idea of the MCNN-RF method is shown in Figure 2. First, the spectral bands of Landsat 8-OLI data are used to calculate the NDVI, NDPI, and NDGI for each season within the year, and to construct the band-index-based multi-temporal data. Next, the training sample points and band-index-based multi-temporal data are used to train the MCNN network for a per-pixel mapping from the 40-dimensional multi-temporal input to wetland class probabilities, and to obtain the MCNN-based wetland category probability. Then, the band-index-based multi-temporal data and the learned deep features are combined with the RF method to obtain the band-index-RF-based wetland category probability and the multi-temporal feature-RF-based wetland category probability, respectively. Finally, the three wetland category probabilities obtained above are multiplied and integrated to obtain the final wetland fine-grained mapping results.
